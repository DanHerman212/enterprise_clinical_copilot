Generated by `evaluation/inventory/holdout_followup_matrix.py`: the deterministic follow-up selector and the driver-bar question template, run over the 10 holdout records against the same tool payloads the commands retrieve. **Zero LLM calls.**

The row holds up to 3 chips; `—` marks a slot the selector left empty. `/meds` runs without the model's extracted medication rows (the matrix cannot produce them without an LLM call), so a regimen chip may name a different pair of drugs live.

Driver questions are the exact string a driver bar sends, produced by `driver_question(humanize(feature))`. A `risk_driver` turn copies one of them rather than rebuilding it.
